# Ground-truth datasets

Every file here is generated by `scripts/make_null_data.py` from a fixed
seed; none is hand-edited. `CHECKSUMS.json` holds the sha1 of each file and
`phack bench check` verifies them. Regenerate with
`python scripts/make_null_data.py --all` (and `--effect` for the positive
controls).

## Data-generating processes

**panel.** Unit effects α_i ~ N(0,1), a random-walk year effect, AR(1) unit
shocks (ρ = 0.5, σ = 0.8), four i.i.d. covariates of which three enter the
outcome, a log-normal population weight, five regions. Treatment is a
persistent within-unit indicator that flips with probability 0.15 per year
(about 53% on). Outcomes: `y`; `y_alt` = 0.7·y + noise; `y_rate`, a
population-scaled nonlinear recode. Effect: `effect · treat`, zero in the
null file.

**staggered.** Five adoption cohorts (2006–2014) plus 30% never-treated;
adopters have a higher level (+0.4, absorbed by unit effects). AR(1) shocks,
x1/x2, population weight, eight regions. Treatment is absorbing.

**rdd.** Running variable ~ U(−1, 1). Conditional mean 0.3 + 0.6x + 0.4x²
(smooth through the cutoff), county effects (50 counties), a covariate `w1`
correlated with the running variable. Effect: a jump of `effect` at 0.

**iv.** A shared shock `e` enters both `d` and `y`, so OLS is biased; `z1`,
`z2` are strong and `z3` is weak by construction (first-stage coefficient
0.03); controls `c1`, `c2`; 30 groups. Effect: `effect · d` (structural).

## Why positive controls

A calibration that only ever says "nothing survives" proves nothing. The
`effect_*` files show the honest pipeline keeping its power: on a true
effect of the stated size the null-calibrated p-value is small and the
pre-registered specification rejects. `scripts/calibrate_engine.py --effect`
reports the power across fresh draws.
